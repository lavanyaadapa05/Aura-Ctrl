AuraCtrl Gesture Control Guide

Aura-Ctrl enables touchless computer interaction using real-time hand gesture recognition. Using your webcam and computer vision, the system detects hand landmarks and converts gestures into mouse and system control actions.

1. Getting Started

Gesture control uses your webcam to detect hand movements. Follow these steps to begin:

  1. Launch the Aura-Ctrl application.
  2. Click Gesture Control on the main screen.
  3. The webcam will activate automatically.
  4. Position your hand clearly in front of the camera.
  5. Ensure your hand is fully visible and well lit.

2. Cursor Movement

The cursor can be controlled by moving your index finger in front of the camera.

  • Raise your index finger.
  • Move your hand slowly to control the cursor.
  • The cursor position corresponds to your finger position.
  • Smooth movements provide better accuracy.

3. Left Click

You can perform a left click using a simple finger gesture.

  • Bring your index finger and thumb close together.
  • Hold briefly and release.
  • The system interprets this gesture as a left mouse click.

4. Drag and Drop

Drag operations can be performed by holding the click gesture.

  • Bring the index finger and thumb very close together.
  • Hold them together to start dragging.
  • Move your hand to drag the object.
  • Separate the fingers to drop the item.

5. Right Click

Right clicking can be triggered using another finger combination.

  • Bring your thumb and pinky finger close together.
  • This performs a right click action.

6. Scrolling

Scrolling allows navigation through web pages or documents.

  • Join your index finger and middle finger.
  • Move your hand up to scroll up.
  • Move your hand down to scroll down.

7. Volume Control

System volume can be controlled using a fist gesture.

  • Close all fingers to form a fist.
  • Move your hand upwards to increase volume.
  • Move your hand downwards to decrease volume.

8. Brightness Control

Screen brightness can be adjusted while maintaining the fist gesture.

  • Keep your hand in fist mode.
  • Move your hand right to increase brightness.
  • Move your hand left to decrease brightness.

9. Stopping Gesture Control

  • Press the X key on your keyboard.
  • Or close the gesture window.

10. Tips for Best Performance

  • Use a plain background.
  • Avoid multiple hands in the camera frame.
  • Maintain consistent lighting.
  • Keep your hand within camera view.
  • Move gestures slowly for better detection accuracy.
