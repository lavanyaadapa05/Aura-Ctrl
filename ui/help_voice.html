AuraCtrl Voice Control Guide

Aura-Ctrl allows you to control applications, system settings, and presentations using voice commands. The system uses speech recognition to convert spoken commands into system actions.

1. Starting Voice Control

  • Open Aura-Ctrl.
  • Click Voice Based Control.
  • The system will activate your microphone.
  • Wait until you hear the message "Listening".
  • Speak commands clearly.

2. Opening Applications

You can launch installed applications using voice commands.

  • "open chrome"
  • "open notepad"
  • "open calculator"
  • "open vscode"

3. Window Management

  • "minimise chrome"
  • "maximize chrome"
  • "close chrome"

4. Window Arrangement

Aura-Ctrl can automatically organize windows on the screen.

  • "arrange left right" → splits two apps side-by-side.
  • "arrange top bottom" → stacks two apps vertically.
  • "swap windows" → swaps positions of active windows.

5. Volume Control

  • "volume up"
  • "volume down"
  • "mute volume"

6. Brightness Control

  • "brightness up"
  • "brightness down"
  • "set brightness to 50 percent"

7. WiFi Control

  • "wifi on"
  • "wi-fi off"

8. Mouse Control

  • "mouse left"
  • "mouse right"
  • "mouse up"
  • "mouse down"
  • "click"
  • "double click"

9. Scrolling

  • "scroll up"
  • "scroll down"

10. Screenshot

  • "take screenshot"
  • The screenshot will be saved automatically in the project folder.

11. PowerPoint Control

  • "create new presentation"
  • "create slide about artificial intelligence"
  • "start presentation"
  • "next slide"
  • "previous slide"
  • "stop presentation"

12. Stopping Voice Control

  • Say "exit"
  • Or say "stop"
